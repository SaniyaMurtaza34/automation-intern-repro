# Cyber Security – Research, Reflection & Task

## Research & Learn

### 1. What are common cyber security threats in a remote work environment?

Working remotely comes with security risks, especially when using personal devices or public Wi-Fi. Some common threats include phishing emails, weak passwords, malware, fake websites, and unauthorised access to company accounts. If we're not careful, these threats can expose sensitive company or user information.

### 2. What are the best practices for keeping your devices and accounts secure?

I think the best way to stay secure is to follow simple security habits every day. This includes using strong passwords, enabling two-factor authentication (2FA), keeping software updated, avoiding suspicious links, and locking devices when not in use. I would also avoid accessing sensitive company information over unsecured public Wi-Fi.

### 3. Why is it important to lock your computer when away from your desk?

Locking my computer prevents other people from accessing my work, personal information, or company data without permission. Even if I'm stepping away for a few minutes, it's important to lock my screen to keep everything secure.

### 4. How should you handle phishing attempts and suspicious links?

If I receive a suspicious email or message, I won't click any links or download attachments. I'll check the sender's email address and verify the message through an official channel. If something looks suspicious, I'll report it to the appropriate team rather than taking any risks.

### 5. What makes a strong password, and why should you use a password manager?

A strong password should be long, unique, and difficult to guess. I would use a password manager because it helps generate and securely store different passwords for each account. This also reduces the risk of reusing passwords or forgetting them.

### 6. Why is two-factor authentication (2FA) important, and when should you enable it?

2FA adds an extra layer of security beyond a password. Even if someone gets my password, they would still need another verification method to access my account. I believe it should be enabled on all important accounts, especially work accounts, email, and any platforms containing sensitive information.

## Reflection

### 1. What security measures do you currently follow, and where can you improve?

I try to follow basic security practices, such as using passwords, keeping my devices updated, and being careful with unknown links. However, I can improve by using a password manager, reviewing my account security settings regularly, and making sure 2FA is enabled wherever possible.

### 2. How can you make secure behaviour a habit rather than an afterthought?

I can make cyber security part of my daily routine by locking my computer whenever I step away, checking links before opening them, and keeping my software updated. I believe small, consistent actions can make a big difference in protecting information.

### 3. What steps will you take to ensure your passwords and accounts are secure?

I'll review my existing passwords, replace any weak or reused ones, and start using a trusted password manager. I'll also enable 2FA on my work accounts and avoid sharing passwords with anyone.

### 4. What would you do if you suspected a security breach or suspicious activity on your account?

If I notice unusual activity, I'll immediately report it to the Focus Bear team or the appropriate security contact. I'll also change my password using a trusted device, review active sessions, and sign out of unfamiliar devices. I would follow the company's security procedures to prevent further damage.

As someone working in software testing, I understand how important it is to protect user information and company data. I want to make security part of my everyday workflow, not something I only think about when there's a problem.

At Focus Bear, I'll do my part by following good security practices, staying alert to suspicious activity, and handling company and user information carefully.
