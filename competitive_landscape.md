# Task: Compare Focus Bear with Other Productivity Apps

## Research and comparison

After researching these apps, I found that they all help people improve productivity, but they approach the problem differently. Some mainly block distractions, while others focus on organising daily activities and building better habits.

| App         | Main purpose                       | What makes it useful                                                                                     |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Focus Bear  | Focus, routines and healthy habits | Combines distraction blocking, guided routines, breaks and productivity support.                         |
| Freedom     | Blocking distractions              | Blocks distracting websites and apps across devices and allows users to schedule focus sessions.         |
| Cold Turkey | Strict distraction blocking        | Helps users stay focused by blocking distracting websites and applications, with strong locking options. |
| Tiimo       | Daily planning and organisation    | Uses visual schedules, reminders and planning tools to make daily tasks easier to manage.                |
| Routinery   | Building routines                  | Guides users through daily routines with step-by-step instructions and timers.                           |

### 1. What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?

I think the biggest difference is that Focus Bear brings several productivity features together instead of focusing on just one area.

For example, Freedom and Cold Turkey mainly help people avoid distractions by blocking websites and applications. Tiimo is more focused on visual planning and organising daily activities, while Routinery helps people follow routines step by step.

Focus Bear combines distraction blocking with morning and evening routines, guided breaks and healthy habit building. It is also designed with neurodivergent people, particularly those with ADHD and autism, in mind.

I particularly like that Focus Bear guides users through one step at a time rather than showing them too many tasks at once. This can make daily activities feel less overwhelming.



### 2. If I were a user, why would I choose Focus Bear over competitors?

If I were choosing a productivity app, I would choose Focus Bear because it offers more than just distraction blocking.

I would find it helpful to have my morning routine, work sessions and breaks organised in one place. I also like that it works across computers and mobile devices, so I can maintain my focus even when switching between devices.

Another reason is its approach to supporting people with ADHD and autism. The guided routines, simple instructions and reminders can make it easier to build consistent habits without feeling overwhelmed.

For me, the biggest advantage is having one app that supports both productivity and a healthier daily routine.



### 3. What's one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't?

One feature I found interesting is Tiimo's AI Co-Planner and visual daily schedule.

Tiimo allows users to enter their tasks and use AI to break larger tasks into smaller steps, estimate how long they might take and organise them into a daily schedule.

I think this is useful because sometimes people know what they need to do but struggle with where to start or how to organise everything.

Focus Bear already provides guided routines and AI distraction detection, but an integrated visual daily planner with AI task breakdown would be a valuable additional feature.



### 4. Based on my research, what's one improvement Focus Bear could make?

One improvement I would suggest is introducing an AI-powered daily planning feature.

For example, users could enter their daily tasks, deadlines and priorities, and the app could suggest a realistic schedule by breaking bigger tasks into smaller, manageable steps.

It could also allow users to adjust their schedule when something unexpected happens, rather than feeling pressured to follow the original plan.

I think this would make Focus Bear even more helpful for people who struggle with time management, task prioritisation and getting started.

It would also make the app more competitive with tools like Tiimo while keeping its existing strengths in distraction blocking and habit building.

### My overall reflection

After comparing these apps, I realised that there is no single productivity tool that is perfect for everyone. Freedom and Cold Turkey are strong options for people who mainly need distraction blocking, while Tiimo and Routinery are useful for planning and building routines.

However, I think Focus Bear has a strong advantage because it combines different aspects of productivity with a focus on neurodivergent users.

One thing I would keep in mind is that adding more features should not make the app complicated. The main goal should be to make users' lives easier, not give them another thing to manage.

My takeaway: Focus Bear should continue building on what makes it unique while listening to user feedback and learning from competitors.
