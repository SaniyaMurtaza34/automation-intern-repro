# Data Privacy & Confidentiality – Focus Bear

## Research & Learn

### 1. What are the key takeaways from Focus Bear’s privacy policy?

After reading Focus Bear’s Privacy Policy, I learned that protecting users’ personal information is a major responsibility.

My key takeaways are:

- Focus Bear collects personal information such as names, email addresses, habits, device details, and optional health-related information.
- User information should only be collected and used for clear and legitimate purposes.
- Sensitive habit data is protected through encryption.
- Users have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal information.
- Focus Bear follows GDPR privacy principles and uses third-party service providers to support its services.



### 2. What types of data are considered confidential at Focus Bear?

I understand that confidential information can include:

- Personal information: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and account details.
- Sensitive user data: Health information, ADHD or autism-related survey responses, and personal habits.
- Financial information: Payment and subscription details.
- Company information: Internal documents, source code, business plans, and project discussions.
- Security information: Passwords, API keys, and system credentials.

I believe all this information should be handled carefully and only accessed when necessary for work.

### 3. What are best practices for handling confidential data?

Some important practices I would follow are:

- Use strong passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
- Never share passwords or sensitive information with unauthorised people.
- Store confidential files only in approved, secure locations.
- Avoid downloading sensitive information onto personal devices.
- Double-check recipients before sharing files or emails.
- Never upload confidential data to public AI tools without company approval.
- Lock my computer whenever I step away.

### 4. How should you respond to a suspected data breach?

If I notice a possible data breach or accidentally share confidential information, I would report it immediately to my supervisor or the appropriate security contact. I would explain what happened, what information may have been exposed, and when the incident occurred. I would also follow the company's instructions to contain the issue, preserve relevant evidence, and avoid trying to hide the mistake.


## Reflection

### 1. What steps can you take to handle data securely in your daily tasks?

I will protect data by using strong passwords, enabling two-factor authentication, and locking my laptop when I'm not using it. As a QA tester, I will use dummy data instead of real user information whenever possible. I will also check my screenshots, test reports, and logs to make sure I'm not exposing sensitive information.

### 2. How should you store, share, and dispose of sensitive information safely?

I will store sensitive information in secure, password-protected locations and avoid saving it on personal devices. Before sharing any files, I will double-check the recipient and make sure the information is safe to share. When data is no longer needed, I will delete it securely according to the required guidelines.

### 3. What common mistakes lead to data privacy issues, and how can they be avoided?

I think many privacy issues happen because of small mistakes, such as sending an email to the wrong person, using weak passwords, leaving a computer unlocked, or accidentally including sensitive data in screenshots. These mistakes can be avoided by being more careful, checking information before sharing it, and following security guidelines. I also believe regular security awareness and good daily habits can make a big difference.
