# Understanding Focus Bear’s Mission & Vision

## 1. Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

I think Focus Bear was created to help people build better habits, improve their focus, and manage their daily routines. It was also designed to support people with ADHD and Autism who may face challenges with concentration, organisation, time management, and maintaining daily routines. In today's world, there are so many distractions, and staying focused and productive can be difficult for anyone. I believe Focus Bear aims to make everyday life easier by providing tools and support that help people develop healthy habits, stay organised, and become more confident and independent in achieving their goals.

## 2. What problem is Focus Bear solving?

Focus Bear is helping people who struggle with distractions, procrastination, time management, and maintaining consistent routines. It also focuses on supporting neurodivergent individuals, including people with ADHD and Autism, by providing a more structured and supportive environment that considers their different needs.

## 3. Why do you think this mission is important?

I think this mission is important because everyone is different. We all have our own ways of thinking, learning, and getting things done. Some people may find it harder to stay focused, manage their time, or keep up with daily responsibilities. Instead of putting pressure on them, I believe it's important to provide the right support and understanding. Focus Bear can help people build better habits, feel more confident, and manage their daily lives in a way that works best for them.

## 4. How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?

I find Focus Bear's mission meaningful because it goes beyond just building software; it focuses on understanding people's different needs and helping them improve their everyday lives. I am particularly interested in learning how accessibility and thoughtful design can make technology more inclusive. I also see this as an opportunity to grow my understanding of how software can support people in their daily routines, focus, and productivity.


## 5. Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

Yes, I can relate to some of these challenges. Sometimes, managing multiple tasks, maintaining focus, and balancing learning with daily responsibilities can be challenging. I have realised that having a clear plan, setting priorities, and following a consistent routine can make a big difference. This also helps me understand why tools like Focus Bear can be valuable for people who need extra support with focus and organisation.

## Do you know someone with ADHD or autism or even someone without a diagnosis who has struggled socially or to perform well at uni? How might Focus Bear help them?

I don't personally know anyone who has been diagnosed with ADHD or Autism, but I understand that people can face challenges with concentration, managing time, staying organised, and completing their daily tasks, even without a diagnosis.

During university and in everyday life, I have realised that managing multiple responsibilities, meeting deadlines, and staying focused can sometimes be challenging. I think Focus Bear could help people by making it easier to build routines, avoid distractions, and break bigger tasks into smaller steps.

What I like about Focus Bear is that it understands everyone is different. Not everyone works or learns in the same way, and I think having the right support can make a big difference in helping people feel more confident and achieve their goals.
