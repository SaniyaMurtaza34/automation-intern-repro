# Internship Reflection Questions

## 1. What do you hope to learn from this internship?

I hope to gain practical experience working in a real team environment, understand how the team manages day-to-day tasks, and learn how to contribute effectively to the product. I'm also interested in understanding more about Focus Bear's mission and how technology can make everyday life easier for neurodivergent individuals. I want to learn from experienced team members, improve my technical knowledge, and become more confident in my role.

## 2. What skills are you most excited to develop?

I'm most excited to improve my automation testing skills, especially with modern tools and AI-driven testing. I also want to develop my problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills. Since I'm interested in how AI can improve software testing, I'd love to explore practical ways to use AI in QA while continuing to strengthen my testing fundamentals.

## 3. What do you think you can contribute to the team?

I can contribute my previous experience in software quality assurance, particularly in manual testing, automation testing, identifying bugs, and improving software quality. I enjoy exploring applications, thinking from a user's perspective, and finding issues that might affect the user experience. I'm also a quick learner, responsible with my tasks, and always willing to support my teammates whenever needed.

## 4. What is one area you feel less confident in that you'd like to improve?

One area I'd like to improve is my confidence in working with unfamiliar tools, technologies, and codebases. Although I have experience with testing tools like Selenium, Playwright, and Postman, I know there is always more to learn. I sometimes need extra time to understand a new system, but I see that as part of learning. I want to become more independent, ask better questions, and feel confident taking on new challenges.

## 5. What does success in this internship look like for you?

For me, success means learning something new, improving my technical skills, and becoming a more confident QA professional. I'd like to become more confident in my responsibilities, understand the product better, identify and communicate issues effectively, and complete my assigned tasks with quality. I want the team to feel that they can rely on me and that my contribution has made a positive difference. Most importantly, I'd like to finish this internship knowing that I've contributed something valuable, grown professionally, and become a better team member.
