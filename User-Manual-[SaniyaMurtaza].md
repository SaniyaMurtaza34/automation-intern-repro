# Creating Your "User Manual for Me"

## Research & Learn

A “User Manual for Me” is a personal guide that explains how someone prefers to work, communicate, collaborate, and receive feedback. Teams use these manuals to understand each other's working styles and create a more inclusive and supportive workplace. Useful categories include communication preferences, work habits, productivity needs, teamwork, feedback, and personal strengths. How it improves teamwork: It helps prevent misunderstandings, encourages open communication, respects individual differences, and makes it easier for teammates to support one another.

## Personal Reflection

I believe I do my best work when expectations are clear and communication is open. I prefer friendly, direct messages and appreciate constructive feedback that helps me improve. Having organised tasks and uninterrupted focus time helps me stay productive. I also understand that everyone has a different working style, so I try to be flexible, respectful, and open to learning from others.


# User Manual for Saniya Murtaza

## My Role

- I am working as an Automation Intern at Focus Bear, where I focus on manual testing, automation testing, finding bugs, and making sure applications work as expected. I'm also interested in learning more about AI and how it can be used in automation testing.
- I work Monday to Friday during my agreed working hours (Melbourne time).

## How I Work Best

- I work best when I know what needs to be done and have clear instructions. I enjoy figuring things out, solving problems, and learning something new while working.
- I sometimes struggle when tasks aren't explained clearly or when priorities change suddenly without any updates.

## Communication Style

- I prefer simple, clear, and friendly messages. I don't mind detailed explanations, especially when I'm learning something new.
- The easiest way to reach me is through team messages or email. I'm also comfortable having a quick call if something is easier to discuss.
- If something is urgent, just send me a direct message and let me know so I can focus on it first.

## Collaboration & Teamwork

- I enjoy working with people who are supportive, open to sharing ideas, and willing to help each other. I believe teamwork becomes easier when everyone feels comfortable asking questions.
- One thing that bothers me is when important updates aren't communicated, especially if they affect the work we're doing.
- If I'm quiet during a meeting, I'm usually listening and trying to understand everything. Sometimes I just need a little time to organise my thoughts before speaking.

## Focus & Productivity

- I like making a to-do list and completing tasks based on their priority. It helps me stay organised and keep track of my progress.
- Too many interruptions or switching between different tasks can sometimes break my focus.
- I work better when I have clear goals, realistic deadlines, and enough time to concentrate on what I'm doing.

## Feedback & Growth

- I appreciate honest and helpful feedback. I prefer when someone explains what I can improve and gives me an example so I can learn from it.
- If I make a mistake, I'd rather someone tell me directly and privately. I'm always happy to correct it and learn how to avoid it next time.
- I really appreciate teammates who share their experiences, help when someone is stuck, and encourage each other to keep learning.

## Fun Facts & Quirks

- Sometimes I ask a lot of questions, but that's usually because I want to understand things properly and make sure I'm doing them the right way.
- I enjoy trying out new testing tools and learning how AI can make testing easier and more effective.
- I think my biggest strength is paying attention to small details. I enjoy finding issues, understanding why they happen, and figuring out how things can be improved.

## A Little More About Me

I'm a curious person who enjoys learning and improving my skills. I don't expect to know everything, and I'm always happy to ask questions or learn from someone else's experience. I like working in a friendly environment where people respect each other and feel comfortable sharing their thoughts. I might take some time to get familiar with something new, but I always try my best to understand it and do a good job. I'm excited to be part of Focus Bear, meet new people, learn from the team, and contribute wherever I can.
