# Roles in an Agile Team

## Research & Learn

### 1. What are the key roles in an Agile team?

- Developers: They design, build, and maintain the software. They work on new features, fix bugs, and make sure the application works as expected.

- QA (Quality Assurance): QA Engineers test the application to find bugs and make sure the software meets the requirements. They work closely with developers to report issues, verify fixes, and help deliver a reliable product.

- Product Manager (PM): The Product Manager understands customer needs, sets product priorities, and decides which features are most important to develop.

- Agile Project Manager (Agile PM): They help the team stay organised, remove obstacles, and ensure work progresses smoothly. In Scrum, the Scrum Master often performs similar responsibilities, although the roles are not exactly the same.

- Other supporting roles: UX designers focus on making the product easy to use, stakeholders share business needs and expectations, and customer support teams provide feedback about users' experiences and problems.

### 2. How do these roles collaborate in Kanban vs. Scrum?

Scrum: The team works in short cycles called sprints, usually lasting one to four weeks. Developers, QA, and the Product Owner work together to plan tasks, track progress, test features, and review completed work. The Scrum Master helps the team follow Scrum practices and overcome obstacles.

Kanban:The team manages work continuously using a board with columns such as To Do, In Progress, Testing, and Done. Team members pick up tasks as capacity becomes available rather than waiting for the next sprint. QA and developers collaborate throughout the process to identify and fix problems quickly.

The main difference: Scrum focuses on working in sprints, while Kanban focuses on continuous workflow. Both encourage communication, teamwork, and delivering quality software.

## Reflection

### What is your role, and how does it contribute to the Agile process?

My role is QA Engineer. I help the team make sure the application works correctly and meets user requirements. I create and execute test cases, perform functional and regression testing, report bugs, and retest fixes before a release. I also participate in Agile meetings and share feedback about potential risks or quality issues.

### How do your responsibilities overlap with other roles?

I work closely with developers because we both want to deliver a reliable product. When I find a bug, I explain the issue clearly, provide steps to reproduce it, and share any useful evidence. Developers investigate and fix the problem, and I test it again to confirm the fix works. I also communicate with the Product Manager to clarify requirements and make sure the features meet the expected outcomes. This collaboration helps us identify issues early and avoid misunderstandings.

### How My Role Fits into the Agile Team

As a QA Engineer, my role in an Agile team is to help deliver reliable, high-quality software. I work with developers and other team members throughout the development process, rather than waiting until the end to test the application. My responsibilities include preparing test cases, performing functional and regression testing, reporting bugs, and verifying fixes. I also share feedback during team meetings to help identify risks and improve product quality.

I frequently collaborate with developers. I believe teamwork can improve when we communicate clearly, discuss issues early, and share enough information to understand problems quickly. I can contribute by providing clear bug reports, asking questions when requirements are unclear, and involving developers in discussions about complex issues.
